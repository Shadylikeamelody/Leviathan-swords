A force of the abyss.

Built for the depths. Relentless in pursuit. The Leviathan grows more dangerous as its health wanes.

01THE HUNT BEGINS

One ancient will. Two deadly phases.

The Leviathan hunts nearby players with crushing close-range strikes, striking every 1.5 seconds. Its 1,000 health is displayed on a custom dark-blue and cyan boss bar. At half health, the creature enrages—its fury does not fade until the fight is over.
